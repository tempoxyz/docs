# Integrate stablecoin payments

Build a payment flow from the first transfer through reconciliation. For the complete collection of transfer features and machine payment integrations, see [Payments](/docs/payments).

<span id="core-payment-flows" />

## Build the payment flow

:::::steps

### Choose an account and token

[Create or connect an account](/docs/accounts) and choose the [stablecoin](/get-started/stablecoins) your application will use. Record the network and token contract address alongside the payment amount and recipient.

### Send a payment

Use the [transfer guide](/docs/guide/payments/send-a-payment) to submit a payment and read its receipt. Add a [memo](/docs/guide/payments/transfer-memos) if the recipient needs an invoice or order reference.

### Confirm receipt

Follow [Receive a payment](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment) to read transfer events and verify the token, amount, and recipient. Handle [blocked receipts](/docs/guide/payments/configure-receive-policies#monitor-blocked-receipts) before treating a successful transaction as delivery.

### Reconcile the payment

Match the memo to your records, or use [customer deposit addresses](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses) to attribute incoming payments while receiving funds in one registered account.

:::::

<span id="payment-controls" />

## Add payment controls

- [Sponsor fees](/docs/guide/payments/sponsor-user-fees) or [choose a supported fee token](/docs/guide/payments/pay-fees-in-any-stablecoin).
- [Batch payments](/docs/guide/payments/send-a-payment#batch-payment-transactions) in one transaction, or [submit independent transactions in parallel](/docs/guide/payments/send-parallel-transactions).
- [Configure receive policies](/docs/guide/payments/configure-receive-policies) to choose which tokens and senders an account accepts.

Use [Routes](/docs/routes) when funds need to move between Tempo and another network.
