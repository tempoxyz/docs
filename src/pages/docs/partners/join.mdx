## Choose your integration path

| You are building | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| An application that holds or sends stablecoins | [Accounts](/docs/accounts) and [account integration](/docs/accounts/integrate) |
| A wallet, exchange, or infrastructure service | [Network configuration](/docs/quickstart/connection-details) and [EVM compatibility](/docs/quickstart/evm-compatibility) |
| A funding or cross-chain transfer integration | [Routes](/docs/routes) and [bridge providers](/docs/partners/bridges) |
| A service for agents to discover and pay for | [Machine Payments](/docs/agents) |

Use the [partner directory](/docs/partners) to find providers by category, including wallets, payment providers, node infrastructure, data, and compliance.

## Discuss your integration with Tempo

[Contact the Tempo team](https://tempo.xyz/contact) with a short description of your integration. Include:

- What your product does and who uses it.
- The Tempo products or partner services you want to connect.
- Your current stage: exploring, prototyping, testing, or preparing to launch.
- The help you need, such as a technical discussion or a provider introduction.

If you already have an implementation, link to its public documentation or repository so the team can understand how it works.
