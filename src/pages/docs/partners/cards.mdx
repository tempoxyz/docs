# Cards

Add card spending to your application through a card issuing partner. Start with the product below that matches how your customers hold and spend funds.

## <span className={`partner-logo partner-logo--bridge ${partnerLogo().className + ' ' + partnerLogoBridge().className}`} aria-hidden="true" />Bridge Cards

Issue stablecoin-backed Visa cards with Bridge and Stripe Issuing. Bridge handles customer onboarding and KYC; Stripe handles card creation, controls, and operations. Funding options include custodial and noncustodial wallets.

[Cards product](https://www.withbridge.com/product/cards) · [Consumer issuing guide](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/cards/overview/stripe-issuing) · [Tempo integration guide](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/get-started/guides/move-money/tempo-integration-guide)

## <span className={`partner-logo partner-logo--wirex ${partnerLogo().className + ' ' + partnerLogoWirex().className}`} aria-hidden="true" />Wirex BaaS

Build a stablecoin card program with Wirex's card issuance APIs. Its Tempo integration uses Tempo as the settlement layer. Follow the card guide to create cardholders and issue virtual or physical cards.

[Card issuance guide](https://docs.wirexapp.com/docs/retail-card-issuance) · [Supported environments](https://docs.wirexapp.com/docs/retail-environments) · [Tempo launch announcement](https://tempo.xyz/blog/wirex-is-now-live-on-tempo/)

## <span className={`partner-logo partner-logo--ur ${partnerLogo().className + ' ' + partnerLogoUr().className}`} aria-hidden="true" />UR Mastercard

Add a Mastercard debit card as part of a UR account integration. Card spending draws from the customer's multi-currency UR account; UR also provides the account and bank transfer infrastructure.

[Mastercard product](https://ur.app/blog/product/multicurrency-mastercard-ur) · [Build with UR on Tempo](https://ur.app/tempo) · [Partner portal](https://partner.ur.app/)

## Connect card spending to your application

Use [Wallets](/docs/partners/wallets) to choose how customers hold funds, and [Payments & ramps](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps) for bank funding and payouts. Confirm supported assets, customer countries, and funding flows with the issuing partner before choosing a program.
