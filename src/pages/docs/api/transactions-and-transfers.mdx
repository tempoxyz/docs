# Transactions and transfers

A transaction is a signed submission to Tempo. A token transfer is a movement of tokens caused by executing a transaction. Query transactions to inspect execution and fees; query transfers to reconcile token movements.

## Tempo transaction and transfer model

One transaction can produce zero, one, or many transfers. For example, a payment may produce one transfer, a swap may produce several, and a contract update may produce none. Each transfer carries the `transactionHash` of the transaction that produced it.

## When to use transactions or transfers

| Record | Endpoint | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction | [`/v1/transactions`](/docs/api/transactions) | Inspect submitted transactions, execution status, and fees. |
| Token transfer | [`/v1/transfers`](/docs/api/transfers) | Identify the sender, recipient, token, and amount for reconciliation. |

A payment is your application's business record. Associate it with the relevant transaction hashes and transfer events. A successful transaction alone does not establish that the intended recipient received the payment; follow [Receive a payment](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment) to verify delivery.

A [Routes transfer](/docs/routes/transfers) tracks a cross-network workflow and has its own ID and delivery status. It can involve transactions on more than one network.
