# Tempo Accounts

Give every customer and business a stablecoin account with security rules that Tempo enforces onchain.

## Why Tempo Accounts

- **Native account abstraction.** Passkey signatures, access keys, batched calls, and [fee sponsorship](/docs/accounts/fees/fee-sponsorship) are built into every [Tempo Transaction](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction), with no wallet contracts, bundlers, or paymasters to run.
- **Self-custodial.** Signing rules live onchain and Tempo checks every transaction against them, so when the customer holds the [passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) or [private key](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys), neither you nor Tempo can move their funds.
- **Your wallet stack works.** Tempo is [EVM compatible](/docs/quickstart/evm-compatibility), so [wallet providers](/docs/accounts/apps) and [custodians](/docs/accounts/custodians) such as Privy, Turnkey, Dynamic, and Fireblocks work, along with browser wallets like MetaMask.

## What you can build

- **Stablecoin accounts for your users.** Each user gets their own account in your app, approved with a key they hold, such as a [passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) or a key from a provider like Privy or Turnkey, so you never hold their funds.
- **Automated payments.** Your backend, card program, or AI agent pays with its own revocable [access key](/docs/accounts/access-keys), and Tempo rejects any payment over its limit, to an unlisted payee, or after it expires.
- **Business accounts.** Hold customer funds in one [omnibus account](/docs/accounts/custodians/omnibus-accounts) with a [deposit address](/docs/accounts/virtual-addresses) per customer and no sweep transactions, or run a treasury from a [custodian](/docs/accounts/custodians) like Fireblocks.

## Account model

A Tempo account is an address that holds stablecoins, with its rules stored onchain:

- **Address:** holds the account's stablecoins and never changes, even when you [replace its signers](/docs/accounts/account-configuration).
- **Signers:** approve payments and changes to the account. Each is a [private key, device key, or passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication) with a weight, and a transaction runs once its approvers' weights reach the [threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds), such as any 2 of 3.
- **Access keys:** let a customer's phone, your backend, or an AI agent [pay on its own](/docs/accounts/access-keys), within the limits, expiry, and allowed calls you set.

## Start building

On mainnet, [create a single-key account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-single-key-account) or support the [wallets your users already have](/docs/accounts/wallets). To try several signers and recovery, [create a configurable account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-configurable-account) on the devnet. Then [add funds](/docs/accounts/fund).
