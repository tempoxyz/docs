## Fund accounts and reconcile payments

Use [OUSD](/docs/guide/ousd) for Tempo stablecoin balances and payments. Follow [wallet funding](/docs/guide/getting-funds) for production or use the separate [testnet faucet](/docs/quickstart/faucet) while developing.

- [Send a payment](/docs/guide/payments/send-a-payment) to transfer stablecoins between accounts.
- [Attach transfer memos](/docs/guide/payments/transfer-memos) to associate transfers with invoices or other records.
- [Use virtual addresses](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses) to attribute customer deposits to a registered wallet. Virtual addresses share that wallet's balance; they do not create a separate balance for each customer.
- Use the [Tempo API](/docs/api) to read payment activity and integrate webhooks in your backend.

## Configure account controls

[Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction) provide fee sponsorship, batching, and access keys for delegated signing. Add the controls your account flow needs:

- [Sponsor user fees](/docs/guide/payments/sponsor-user-fees) or [set fee token preferences](/docs/guide/payments/pay-fees-in-any-stablecoin).
- [Configure receive policies](/docs/guide/payments/configure-receive-policies) to control accepted tokens and senders, including recovery of blocked receipts.
- [Authorize access keys](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction#access-keys) for delegated transaction signing.
- [Send parallel transactions](/docs/guide/payments/send-parallel-transactions) when your application submits concurrent payments.

For chain configuration and execution behavior, see [Integrate Tempo](/docs/quickstart/integrate-tempo). For custom account implementations, compare [smart account providers](/docs/ecosystem/smart-contract-libraries) and the [Tempo Accounts SDK documentation](https://accounts.tempo.xyz/docs).
