# Tempo Accounts

Give every customer a stablecoin account with its security rules built in. Customers approve payments with a passkey, your backend and agents pay within limits you set, and Tempo enforces every rule onchain.

## What you can build

### Accounts customers control

Customers approve payments with a [passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys), a [key on their device](/docs/accounts/authentication/device-keys), or a [private key](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys), and one account can use several. Start with a single passkey, add a backup key so a lost phone doesn't lock anyone out, or pair your wallet provider's key with the customer's so neither of you can move funds alone. When the customer holds the keys, the funds are theirs.

### Automated payments

Your backend, card program, or AI agent can pay from an account with its own [access key](/docs/accounts/access-keys). You set how much it can spend, who it can pay, and when it expires, and Tempo rejects anything outside those limits. A leaked key can only spend what you allowed, and you can revoke it at any time.

### Configurable account policies

Set the rules for money coming in and going out. A [team multisig](/docs/accounts/custodians/team-multisig) needs two approvals for every payment. A [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) accepts only the stablecoins and senders you choose. [Deposit addresses](/docs/accounts/virtual-addresses) give each customer their own address that pays into one account. Fees are paid in stablecoins, and you can [cover them for your customers](/docs/accounts/fees/fee-sponsorship).

### Works with the wallets you already use

Tempo is [fully EVM compatible](/docs/quickstart/evm-compatibility), so accounts work with every popular [wallet provider](/docs/accounts/apps) and [custodian](/docs/accounts/custodians), including Privy, Turnkey, Dynamic, and Fireblocks, and with browser wallets like MetaMask. Keep your current setup and add Tempo's account features on top.

## How Tempo Accounts work

A Tempo account is an address that holds stablecoins, with its rules stored onchain:

- **Address:** holds the account's stablecoins and never changes, even when you [replace its signers](/docs/accounts/account-configuration).
- **Signers:** approve payments and changes to the account. Each is a [private key, device key, or passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication) with a weight, and a transaction runs once its approvers' weights reach the [threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds), such as any 2 of 3.
- **Access keys:** let a customer's phone, your backend, or an AI agent [pay on its own](/docs/accounts/access-keys), within the limits, expiry, and allowed calls you set. A leaked key can't spend past them.

Tempo checks these rules on every transaction, and no one, including Tempo, can override them. Whoever holds the signing keys holds the funds: the customer when they hold a passkey, or a wallet provider or your backend when it holds the key. [Apps](/docs/accounts/apps) and [Custodians](/docs/accounts/custodians) cover each setup, and [Security](/docs/accounts/security) covers who can move funds in each.

On mainnet and testnet today, an account has one key, like a standard Ethereum account, and that key authorizes its access keys. A [configurable account](/docs/development/configurable-accounts) can have several signers, and a single-key account can [upgrade in place](/docs/accounts/account-configuration/account-upgrade) to one, keeping its address and balance.

## Start building

:::info
Single-key accounts, including passkeys, plus access keys, fee tokens, fee sponsorship, receive policies, and virtual addresses work on mainnet and testnet today. Configurable accounts, with several signers and a threshold, are planned for the T14 network upgrade; until then they run only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet).
:::

To build on mainnet today, [create a single-key account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-single-key-account) with a passkey or private key, or support the [wallets your users already have](/docs/accounts/wallets). To try several signers and recovery, [create a configurable account](/docs/accounts/create) on the Tempo Accounts devnet. Then [add funds](/docs/accounts/fund). [Use cases](/docs/accounts/use-cases) matches each setup to [Apps](/docs/accounts/apps), [Automations](/docs/accounts/automations), [Custodians](/docs/accounts/custodians), and [Wallets](/docs/accounts/wallets). Each guide below walks through one setup:
