# Accounts

A Tempo account is a stablecoin address whose signers, approval rules, and spending limits live in the protocol. Tempo enforces them on every transaction, so your customers hold their own funds.

[How it works →](/docs/accounts/how-it-works)

</DocsProductOverview>

:::danger
Tempo Accounts is in early development and runs only on Tempo devnets. These pages are for demonstration. [Contact Tempo](https://tempo.xyz/contact) if you're interested.
:::

## Tempo account building blocks

- **Address:** the account's public identifier, which holds its TIP-20 balances and never changes.
- **Signers:** private keys, device keys, and passkeys that approve, each with a weight toward a threshold.
- **Access keys:** keys on devices, servers, and agents that pay on their own within spending limits, an expiry, and scopes.

Already have single-key accounts? [Upgrade them in place](/docs/accounts/key-management/account-upgrade), at the same address and balance.
