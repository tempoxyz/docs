## Accounts and wallets

An **account** holds token balances at an address. A **wallet** is software or a service that manages signing and requests signatures. Your application's customer record identifies the person or business using that account.

| Who authorizes payments? | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Your backend | [Create an account](/docs/accounts/create) with your signing infrastructure or a [wallet provider](/docs/accounts/providers). |
| A customer inside your app | Use an [embedded wallet provider](/docs/accounts/providers) or create a [passkey account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-passkey-account). |
| A customer with an existing wallet | [Connect a wallet](/docs/accounts/integrate). |

Associate account addresses with the customers or businesses they serve. Your application maintains customer identity and payment records; Tempo records balances and transfers. The [integration walkthrough](/docs/accounts/integration) shows how those pieces fit together.

## Keys and permissions

An account's root key controls signing authority. Grant a service, session, or agent a limited access key when it needs to act on the account. Set the expiry, token budget, and allowed calls for that task.

[Keys and permissions](/docs/accounts/keys) explains how to sign transactions, grant access, and revoke it. Account keys authorize transactions; Tempo API credentials authorize API requests. Neither replaces your application's customer login.

Plan recovery with your wallet or signing provider. The root public key determines the account address; adding or revoking an access key does not replace it.

<span id="understand-account-visibility" />

Balances and transfers on Tempo's public ledger are publicly readable. For private balances and activity, review [Zones](/docs/zones/privacy), including what remains visible to the operator and on the public ledger.
