# Tempo Accounts

Give every customer and business a stablecoin account with security rules that Tempo enforces onchain.

## Overview

A Tempo account is an address that holds stablecoins. Its rules live onchain, and each account has three parts:

- **Address:** Holds the account's stablecoins. It never changes, even when you [replace its signers](/docs/accounts/account-configuration).
- **Signers:** Approve payments and changes to the account. Each is a [private key, device key, or passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication) with a weight. A transaction runs once its approvers' weights reach the [threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds), such as any 2 of 3.
- **Access Keys:** Let a customer's phone, your backend, or an AI agent [pay on its own](/docs/accounts/access-keys). Each works within the limits, expiry, and allowed calls you set.

## Why Tempo Accounts

- **Native Account Abstraction:** Every [Tempo Transaction](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction) has passkey signatures, access keys, batched calls, and [fee sponsorship](/docs/accounts/fees/fee-sponsorship) built in. You run no wallet contracts, bundlers, or paymasters.
- **Self-Custodial:** Signing rules live onchain, and Tempo checks every transaction against them. When the customer holds the [passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) or [private key](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys), neither you nor Tempo can move their funds.
- **Your Wallet Stack Works:** Tempo is [EVM compatible](/docs/quickstart/evm-compatibility). [Wallet providers](/docs/accounts/apps) and [custodians](/docs/accounts/custodians) such as Privy, Turnkey, Dynamic, and Fireblocks work, and so do browser wallets like MetaMask.

## What You Can Build

- **Stablecoin Accounts for Your Users:** Each user gets their own account in your app. They approve with a key they hold, such as a [passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) or a key from a provider like Privy or Turnkey. You never hold their funds.
- **Automated Payments:** Your backend, card program, or AI agent pays with its own revocable [access key](/docs/accounts/access-keys). Tempo rejects any payment over its limit, to an unlisted payee, or after it expires.
- **Business Accounts:** Hold customer funds in one [omnibus account](/docs/accounts/custodians/omnibus-accounts), with a [deposit address](/docs/accounts/virtual-addresses) per customer and no sweep transactions. Or run a treasury from a [custodian](/docs/accounts/custodians) like Fireblocks.

## Start Building

On mainnet, [create a single-key account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-single-key-account) or support the [wallets your users already have](/docs/accounts/wallets). To try several signers and recovery, [create a configurable account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-configurable-account) on the devnet. Then [add funds](/docs/accounts/fund).
