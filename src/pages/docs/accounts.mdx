# Accounts

A Tempo account is a stablecoin address with its rules built in, so whoever holds its keys holds the funds.

[How it works →](/docs/accounts/how-it-works)

</DocsProductOverview>

Each Tempo account holds a customer's stablecoins at one address, with rules that set which keys approve its payments and how much a server or agent can spend on its own. Tempo checks those rules on every transaction and can't override them.

:::warning
Tempo Accounts is in early development. Configurable accounts, with several signers and a threshold, run only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet), not on mainnet or testnet. [Contact Tempo](https://tempo.xyz/contact) to discuss access.
:::

The customer holds their own funds when they hold the signing key, such as a passkey. When a wallet provider or your backend holds the key, that party can move the funds too: see [Apps](/docs/accounts/apps) and [Custodians](/docs/accounts/custodians).

## What's in a Tempo account

- **Address:** holds the account's stablecoins and never changes, even when you [replace its signers](/docs/accounts/account-configuration).
- **Signers:** approve payments and changes to the account. Each is a [private key, device key, or passkey](/docs/accounts/authentication) with a weight. A transaction runs once its approvers' weights add up to the [threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds), such as any 2 of 3.
- **Access keys:** let a customer's phone, your backend, or an AI agent [pay on its own](/docs/accounts/access-keys), within the spending limits, expiry, and allowed calls you set. A leaked key still can't spend past them.

Each transaction pays a network fee in a USD stablecoin, not a separate gas token, and a [fee payer](/docs/accounts/fees) can pay it for your customers. On mainnet and testnet today, use a single-key account (EOA), like a standard Ethereum account: one key, such as a passkey, approves its payments and authorizes its access keys. On the devnet, it can [upgrade in place](/docs/accounts/account-configuration/account-upgrade) to signers and a threshold and keep its address and balance.

## Start building

To build on mainnet today, [create a single-key account](/docs/accounts/create#create-a-single-key-account) with a passkey or private key, or support the [wallets your users already have](/docs/accounts/wallets). To try several signers and recovery, [create a configurable account](/docs/accounts/create) on the Tempo Accounts devnet. Then [add funds](/docs/accounts/fund). [Use cases](/docs/accounts/use-cases) matches each setup to [Apps](/docs/accounts/apps), [Automations](/docs/accounts/automations), [Custodians](/docs/accounts/custodians), and [Wallets](/docs/accounts/wallets), or pick a guide:
