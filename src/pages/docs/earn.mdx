## How Earn works

A vault accepts stablecoin deposits and issues shares representing an account's position. The value of those shares changes with the underlying strategy's returns, losses, and fees. Your app shows the position's current asset value as an earning balance.

<span id="liquidity-and-withdrawals" />
<span id="track-deposits-and-withdrawals" />

Choose a vault by its accepted asset, eligibility, strategy, and withdrawal behavior. Position value and the amount immediately available to withdraw can differ. [How Earn works](/docs/earn/how-it-works) explains the vault and share model.
