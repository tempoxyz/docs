<span id="how-earning-balances-work" />

## How Earn works

A vault accepts stablecoin deposits and issues shares representing each account's holding, called a position. The value of those shares changes with the underlying strategy's returns, losses, and fees.

Read [How Earn works](/docs/earn/how-it-works) for the vault and share model. A position's value and the amount immediately available to withdraw can differ.

## Liquidity and withdrawals

Keep position value, available withdrawals, and pending withdrawals distinct. Follow [Withdraw funds](/docs/earn/withdraw) for immediate and queued redemption flows.

<span id="read-vaults-and-positions" />

## Read positions and earnings

Use the [Earn API](/docs/api/earn) to read positions, share prices, and earnings. [Balances and earnings](/docs/earn/balances) explains how to display them without treating deposits or withdrawals as investment returns.

<span id="connect-earning-balances-to-payments" />

## Use earning balances for payments

Redeem the required stablecoins, confirm their arrival, then [send a payment](/docs/guide/payments/send-a-payment). Pending withdrawals are not available to spend.

<span id="for-yield-providers" />

## Connect a yield source

An engine connects a vault to its underlying yield strategy. If you provide that strategy, implement the engine interface to connect it to Earn. Define the accepted asset, share valuation, fees, access controls, and deposit and redemption behavior. Confirm the engine interface and deployment with Tempo before integrating a new source.

For a [Zone](/docs/zones) integration, check vault support and [private Earn tracking](/docs/earn/integrate#track-private-earn-activity) before using the public-account flow.
