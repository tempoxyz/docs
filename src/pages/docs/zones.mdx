## Overview

A Zone is a private stablecoin ledger connected to Tempo. Use one when other Tempo users shouldn't see your customers' balances and payments, such as payroll or treasury accounts.

:::warning
Zones is in limited preview. [Contact Tempo](https://tempo.xyz/contact) to discuss access and the deployment your application needs.
:::

Each Zone runs its own network. Its portal (`ZonePortal`) on Tempo holds the backing assets. Each account reads only its own balances and activity. The operator can read the whole ledger, and deposit and withdrawal amounts stay public. Read [Privacy](/docs/zones/privacy) before you choose a deployment.

With a Zone, your application can:

- **Fund Private Accounts:** Deposit stablecoins from Tempo into an account in the Zone.
- **Read Private Activity:** Read an account's balances and transfers with its authorization token.
- **Withdraw to Tempo:** Return funds to a Tempo address, or call a contract on Tempo.

The current permissioned runtime moves tokens only through deposits and withdrawals. It disables general TIP-20 transfers and user contract deployment. Confirm the assets, signing tools, read access, and funding rules with the operator. Execution-proof enforcement depends on the deployed verifier and network configuration, as [proving and settlement](/docs/protocol/zones/proving) describes.
