# Tempo Zones

Keep stablecoin balances and account activity in a private ledger connected to Tempo. The operator can read the ledger; deposit and withdrawal amounts remain public.

[Connect to a Zone →](/docs/zones/connect)

</DocsProductOverview>

## Understand the privacy boundary

Use a Zone when customer balances or treasury activity should remain off the public ledger. Decide which accounts your application controls and who needs access to their balances and history. Review the [privacy model](/docs/zones/privacy) for what account holders, the operator, and public observers can see.

## Check the capabilities of your deployment

The current permissioned runtime restricts token movement to the inbox and outbox. General TIP-20 transfers and user contract deployment are disabled. Check the operator's supported interfaces and [execution rules](/docs/protocol/zones/execution).

Execution-proof enforcement depends on the deployed verifier and network configuration. Review [proving and settlement](/docs/protocol/zones/proving) with the operator; forced exits are not a generally available recovery path.

## Try the testnet sandbox

The [interactive sandbox](/docs/guide/private-zones) uses earlier interfaces and capabilities, including plaintext deposits and direct transfers. Use its guides with their named deployment. For another Zone, start with its operator-provided configuration.
