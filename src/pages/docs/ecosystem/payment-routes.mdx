# Supported payment routes

Search live fiat, cross-chain, and exchange routes into and out of Tempo. Filter by source, destination, currency, provider, or route type.

<PaymentRoutesExplorer />

:::warning
Route availability can depend on country, account, asset, amount, compliance checks, liquidity, and provider configuration. Confirm a live quote and the destination token contract with the provider before moving funds. Currency symbols in the table follow each provider's naming.
:::

## How this catalog is maintained

The catalog includes routes that providers publicly describe as live. A provider's general Tempo integration is not enough to add a route: the source, destination, and currencies must also be documented. Announced, planned, prospecting, nurture, and closed-lost integrations are excluded.

Transaction minimums, limits, and settlement estimates are provider-supplied and may change. The catalog was last reviewed on August 22, 2026.
