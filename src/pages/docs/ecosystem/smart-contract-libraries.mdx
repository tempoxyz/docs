# Smart accounts

Choose a provider for account abstraction or programmable smart wallets. Use these integrations when your application needs the provider's account model or existing wallet infrastructure.

Review [Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction) for native batching, fee sponsorship, and access keys. The [wallet integration guide](/docs/quickstart/wallet-developers) explains Tempo-specific transaction and balance handling.

## Pimlico

[Pimlico](https://www.pimlico.io) provides smart account infrastructure for Tempo, including ERC-4337 bundlers and paymasters. With Pimlico, developers can sponsor gas fees, accept ERC-20 tokens for gas, and relay smart account transactions.

Get started on the [Pimlico dashboard](https://dashboard.pimlico.io/) and explore the [Pimlico docs](https://docs.pimlico.io/).

## Safe

[Safe](https://safe.global) provides smart contract accounts with multiple owners and signature thresholds. Its transaction service documents support for [Tempo](https://docs.safe.global/core-api/transaction-service-reference/tempo) and [Moderato testnet](https://docs.safe.global/core-api/transaction-service-reference/tempo-moderato). Use Safe's documentation to check the contracts and modules required by your integration.

## ZeroDev

[ZeroDev](https://zerodev.app) provides a smart account platform for Tempo, supporting both ERC-4337 and EIP-7702. Developers can onboard users with social logins, enable gas sponsorship, and automate transactions while taking advantage of ZeroDev's chain-abstracted workflows. Its modular wallet stack also allows teams to build customized features such as custom transaction policies and tailored approval logic.

Create a project in the [ZeroDev dashboard](https://dashboard.zerodev.app) and follow the [SDK quickstart](https://docs.zerodev.app/sdk/getting-started/quickstart) to integrate smart accounts into your Tempo application.
