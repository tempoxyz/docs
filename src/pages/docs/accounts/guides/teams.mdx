# Teams

These guides set up a company treasury that several people approve, give teams budgets they spend without asking the approvers, and remove an approver who leaves. Funds stay at one address throughout, and no single person can move them alone.

A Tempo account is an address that holds stablecoins. Its signers are the keys allowed to approve payments and changes, each with a weight, and the threshold is the total weight a payment needs. With three approvers at weight 1 and a threshold of 2, any two approve a payment and no one approves alone. [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) explains the model.
