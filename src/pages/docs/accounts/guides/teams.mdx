# Teams

Keep your company's stablecoins in one treasury account that no one can spend from alone.

Each guide here works with one company treasury: a configurable account with several signers, where a payment runs only when enough approvers sign. Use them to set up the treasury, give a team its own daily budget from it, or remove an approver who leaves, all without moving the funds to a new address. Configurable accounts work only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet), not on mainnet or testnet, so every guide here runs there.

[Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) explains signers and thresholds. [Use cases](/docs/accounts/use-cases) compares the setups.

## Choose a guide

| Scenario | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Several people must approve each payment from one company balance | [Set up a company treasury](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/company-treasury) |
| A team pays from the treasury up to a daily limit, without asking the approvers each time | [Give a team a budget](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/team-budget) |
| An approver leaves the company or changes roles | [Offboard an approver](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/offboard-an-approver) |
