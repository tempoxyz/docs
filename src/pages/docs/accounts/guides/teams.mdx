# Teams

Keep your company's stablecoins in one treasury account that no one can spend from alone.

You can change who can spend without moving the funds to a new address. [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) explains signers and thresholds. [Use cases](/docs/accounts/use-cases) compares the setups.

Every guide here uses configurable accounts, which work only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet), not on mainnet or testnet.

## Choose a guide

| Scenario | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Several people must approve each payment from one company balance | [Set up a company treasury](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/company-treasury) |
| A team pays from the treasury up to a daily limit, without asking the approvers each time | [Give a team a budget](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/team-budget) |
| An approver leaves the company or changes roles | [Offboard an approver](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/offboard-an-approver) |
