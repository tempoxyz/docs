# Users

These guides give each of your users a Tempo account they can use, recover, and eventually take with them, while the user keeps a key your app never holds. Pick the scenario that matches your product; each guide walks it end to end.

A Tempo account is an address that holds the user's stablecoins. Its signers are the keys allowed to approve payments and changes, each with a weight, and the threshold is the total weight an approval needs. Tempo checks every approval against them, so your app can only do what those signers allow. [How Tempo Accounts work](/docs/accounts/how-it-works) explains the model.
