# Hand off to a self-custodial wallet

Give a user full control of their account by replacing your app's signers with one they hold in their own wallet, such as Tempo Wallet. The account keeps its address and balance, and your app keeps no key.

:::warning
**Not live yet.** Configurable accounts ([TIP-1107](https://tips.sh/1107)) run only on Tempo devnet, and behavior may change.
:::

<span id="account-export" />
<span id="hand-off-to-the-customer" />
<span id="hand-off-to-a-self-custodial-wallet" />
<span id="why-a-signer-handoff-instead-of-a-private-key-export" />

Your app holds two kinds of keys on the account: signers, which approve payments and account changes, and access keys, which pay within limits. A handoff removes both: your app's signer revokes the access keys, then approves its own replacement by the user's signer. Unlike exporting a private key, a handoff leaves your app no copy, and it works for passkeys and device keys, which can't be exported.

The demo plays your app. It holds the account's only signer and an access key, then hands the account to a device key in this browser, standing in for the user's passkey.

:::::steps

### Get the user's signer address

The user creates a passkey in a self-custodial wallet, such as [Tempo Wallet](https://wallet.tempo.xyz), and sends you its address, `userSigner` below. Have them confirm it in their wallet before you continue: Tempo can't check that the user holds the key behind an address, and a wrong address locks the account.

<span id="handoff-limits" />

### Revoke your app's keys

Changing signers leaves access keys active, so revoke each one your app holds first, while your app's signer can still approve. [Revoked](/docs/accounts/access-keys/revocation) keys can't be authorized on this account again.

```ts [hand-off.ts]
import { Account } from 'viem/tempo'

const appSigner = Account.fromSecp256k1(process.env.APP_SIGNER_KEY as `0x${string}`)
// The address and config you saved. Until the first signer update, the config derives the address.
const account =
  savedConfig.version === 0n
    ? Account.fromConfig({ ...savedConfig, address: 'infer' })
    : Account.fromConfig({ address, ...savedConfig })

for (const accessKey of appAccessKeys) { // the access key addresses you saved
  await client.accessKey.revokeSync({ accessKey, account, owner: appSigner })
}
```

`client` is the devnet client with `Relay.accounts` from [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds#collect-approvals).

<span id="swap-the-signers-in-one-update" />

### Swap the signers

In one update, replace your app's signers, including any key your wallet provider holds for the user, with the user's signer. Your app's signer approves its own removal.

```ts [hand-off.ts]
await client.accounts.updateConfigSync({
  account,
  nextConfig: {
    owners: [{ owner: userSigner, weight: 1 }],
    threshold: 1,
  },
  owner: appSigner,
})
```

If the user loses their only signer, nobody, including Tempo, can recover the account. To leave them a backup, send the [customer accounts setup](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds#recommended-signer-setups) in this update instead: the user's passkey approves alone, and two backup signers they choose approve only together.

### Send the user to their wallet

The user's signer now controls the account at `address`, and the balance is already there. To request payments from them later, [connect your app to their wallet](/docs/accounts/guides/users/embed-wallets#connect-tempo-wallet).
