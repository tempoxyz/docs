# Custodians

Hold stablecoins for your customers or your team, yourself or through a custodian.

This is for exchanges and payments processors that hold many customers' funds, for businesses whose funds sit with a custodian such as Fireblocks, and for teams that want no single person able to move their stablecoins.

## How it works

When a custodian holds your funds, the custody address is a [single-key account](/docs/accounts/how-it-works#address): the custodian holds its key and decides, through its own policies and approvals, which transactions it signs. Plain stablecoin transfers work as on any EVM chain. Fee tokens, fee sponsorship, and access keys need your custodian to send [Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction), so ask which it supports.

When you hold the keys yourself, the account's signers decide. A [team multisig](/docs/accounts/custodians/team-multisig) requires several approvers and keeps its address when they change. It's a configurable account, which until T14 runs only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet).
