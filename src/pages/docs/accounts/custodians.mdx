# Custodians

Hold stablecoins for your customers or your team, yourself or through a custodian.

## Overview

A custody setup decides who holds the keys to stablecoins you hold for others. Either a custodian holds them, or your own approvers do.

Start here if any of these describe you:

- You run an exchange or payments processor that holds many customers' funds.
- Your funds sit with a custodian, such as Fireblocks.
- Your team wants no single person able to move its stablecoins.

## Custody Setups

| Setup | Account | Who holds the keys | Who approves a payment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A custodian holds your funds | [Single-key account](/docs/accounts/how-it-works#address) | The custodian | The custodian, through its own policies and approvals |
| You hold the keys | [Team multisig](/docs/accounts/custodians/team-multisig), a configurable account | Your approvers | Approvers whose weights reach the threshold |

Plain stablecoin transfers from a custody address work as on any EVM chain. Fee tokens, fee sponsorship, and access keys need your custodian to send [Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction). Ask your custodian which it supports before you plan on them for a custody address.

A team multisig keeps its address when its approvers change. Until [T14](/docs/protocol/upgrades/t14), it runs only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet).

For customer deposits, an [omnibus account](/docs/accounts/custodians/omnibus-accounts) gives each customer a deposit address that pays into one account. [Custody partners](/docs/partners/wallets#custodial--institutional), such as Fireblocks, support Tempo.

## Tempo Features

- **[Fee Tokens](/docs/accounts/fees/fee-tokens):** Tempo has no gas token. Set a default stablecoin for fees on each address that sends.
- **[Virtual Addresses](/docs/accounts/virtual-addresses):** Give each customer a deposit address that pays straight into your account, with no sweeps.
- **[Receive Policies](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies):** Accept only the stablecoins and senders you allow. Tempo holds anything else instead of crediting it.
- **[Transfer Memos](/docs/guide/payments/transfer-memos):** Add a payment reference to each payment, so you can reconcile it with your records.
