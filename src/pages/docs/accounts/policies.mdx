# Account Policies

Enforces onchain who can move an account's funds and who can pay it.

## Overview

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces onchain for an account, so your app doesn't have to.

A Tempo account has two policies, one for each direction money moves. A [signing threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) covers funds going out. A [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) covers payments coming in.

## Policy Checks

Tempo checks each policy onchain:

- **Outgoing Funds:** Tempo checks the signing threshold on every transaction the account sends.
- **Incoming Funds:** Tempo checks the receive policy on every transfer and mint to the account.

No one can skip either check, including your app, your wallet provider, Tempo, a breached backend, or a buggy client.

## Combined Policies

The two policies work independently. Without a receive policy, an account accepts every transfer the token itself allows. Add one only when the account must refuse some stablecoins or senders.

Spending limits aren't a policy. They belong to [access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys), which pay alone within their limits and never count toward the threshold.
