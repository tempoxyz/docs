# Account policies

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces onchain for an account, so your app doesn't have to.

A Tempo account has two policies, one for each direction money moves: a signing threshold for funds going out, and a receive policy for payments coming in.

## How it works

Tempo checks an account's policies on every transaction, before it lands. Your app, a wallet provider, and Tempo can't skip them, and neither can a breached backend or a buggy client.

The two policies work independently. Without a receive policy, an account accepts every transfer the token itself allows, so add one only when the account must refuse some stablecoins or senders. Spending limits aren't a policy: they belong to [access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys), which pay alone within their limits and never count toward the threshold.
