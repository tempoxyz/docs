# Account policies

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces onchain for an account, so your app doesn't have to.

A Tempo account has two policies, one for each direction money moves: a signing threshold for funds going out, and a receive policy for payments coming in.

## What a policy is

Tempo checks an account's policies on every transaction, before it lands. Neither your app, a wallet provider, nor Tempo can skip them, so a breached backend or a buggy client can't get past them either.

## Combine policies with access keys

Add a receive policy only when an address must refuse some stablecoins or senders. It checks what arrives, not who approves, so it works alongside any signing threshold.

Spending limits belong to [access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys), not policies. An access key pays alone, within its limits, and never counts toward the threshold.
