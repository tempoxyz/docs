# Policies

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces onchain for an account, so your app doesn't have to. There are two, one for each direction money moves.

## What a policy controls

| Policy | Decides | Available on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) | Who can move funds out and change signers. A transaction needs approvals from signers whose weights add up to the threshold. | [Configurable accounts](/docs/accounts/how-it-works#address), devnet only |
| [Receive policies](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) | Which stablecoins and senders can pay the address. Tempo holds blocked payments for recovery instead of rejecting them. | Mainnet and testnet |

Add a receive policy only when an address must refuse some stablecoins or senders. It checks what arrives, not who approves, so it works alongside any signing threshold.

Spending limits belong to [access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys), not policies. An access key pays alone, within its limits, and never counts toward the threshold.
