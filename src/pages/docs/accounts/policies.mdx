# Policies

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces on every transaction for an account, so your app doesn't have to. Start with a signing threshold, which decides who can move funds; add a receive policy when the account must refuse some tokens or senders.

<span id="what-account-policies-control" />

## What a policy controls

Each policy answers one question about the account, and Tempo checks it on chain before any funds move:

- **Who can approve a payment.** A [signing threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) sets how much signer weight a transaction needs. Whoever holds signers that reach it controls the account.
- **What can arrive.** A [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) decides which stablecoins and senders the address accepts, and holds the rest for recovery.

[Access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys) are a separate control: they pay alone, within spending limits, instead of approving.

## Tempo account policies

| Policy | Controls | Tempo enforces | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) | Outgoing transactions and signer changes | Approving signer weights reach the threshold | Not live yet |
| [Receive policies](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) | Incoming TIP-20 transfers and mints | Token and sender allowlists; blocked payments go to `ReceivePolicyGuard` | Available |

<span id="combine-account-policies" />

## Combine policies

Every account has a signing threshold. Add a receive policy for a specific need:

- **Treasury that pays vendors.** Two of three approvers for every payment, and a team budget as an access key for routine spending.
- **Customer account.** A passkey at weight 2, and a backup key and a recovery provider's key at weight 1 each, at threshold 2, so no provider approves alone.
- **Deposit address.** A receive policy that accepts only the stablecoins you settle in, or only senders you've verified. It works with any threshold, because it checks what arrives, not who approves.

For walkthroughs, see [Set up a company treasury wallet](/docs/accounts/guides/company-treasury) and [Set up self-custody](/docs/accounts/guides/self-custody).
