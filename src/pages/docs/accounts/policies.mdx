# Policies

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces onchain for an account, so your app doesn't have to. Two are available, one for each direction money moves, and two more are experimental.

## What a policy controls

| Policy | Decides | Available on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) | Who can move funds out and change signers. A transaction needs approvals from signers whose weights add up to the threshold. | [Configurable accounts](/docs/accounts/how-it-works#address) on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet) only |
| [Receive policies](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) | Which stablecoins and senders can pay the address. Tempo holds blocked payments for recovery instead of rejecting them. | Any account on mainnet and testnet |
| [Escalation rules](/docs/accounts/policies/escalation-rules) | How much more signer weight larger payments, or payments from a larger balance, need. | Experimental: not on any network yet |
| [Private policies](/docs/accounts/policies/private-policies) | Which signers approve, without revealing them onchain. Tempo checks a zero-knowledge proof that the hidden rule approved. | Experimental: not on any network yet |

Add a receive policy only when an address must refuse some stablecoins or senders. It checks what arrives, not who approves, so it works alongside any signing threshold.

Spending limits belong to [access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys), not policies. An access key pays alone, within its limits, and never counts toward the threshold.
