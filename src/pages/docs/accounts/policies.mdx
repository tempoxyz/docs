# Account policies

A policy is a rule Tempo enforces onchain for an account, so your app doesn't have to.

A Tempo account has two policies, one for each direction money moves. A [signing threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds) decides who can move funds out, and a [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) decides which stablecoins and senders can pay in.

Add a receive policy only when an address must refuse some stablecoins or senders. It checks what arrives, not who approves, so it works alongside any signing threshold.

Spending limits belong to [access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys), not policies. An access key pays alone, within its limits, and never counts toward the threshold.
