# Add funds

A Tempo account can receive stablecoins at its address as soon as it exists.

Pick the path by where the money starts:

| The money is… | How it gets to the account |
| --- | --- |
| In another Tempo account | The sender [transfers it to the address](#send-from-another-tempo-account) |
| On a card or in a bank account | The user [adds it with Tempo Wallet](#add-money-with-tempo-wallet), or your app [embeds an onramp](#add-a-card-or-bank-onramp-to-your-app) |
| On another chain | [Tempo Routes](#bring-stablecoins-from-another-chain) moves it to Tempo |
| Test money, for development | A [faucet](#get-test-funds) sends it |

Whichever path you use, [confirm the funds arrived](#confirm-the-funds-arrived) before you credit them.

## Send from another Tempo account

A sender needs only the account's address. Share it, and the sender transfers stablecoins to it from any Tempo account or wallet.

<span id="add-funds-with-tempo-wallet" />

## Add money with Tempo Wallet

[Tempo Wallet](https://wallet.tempo.xyz) includes a fiat onramp and funding from other chains. Select **Connect Tempo Wallet** below, then **Add funds**, and choose a funding method in the wallet. The wallet shows the available methods, amount, and any fees before you confirm. This demo uses **Tempo Mainnet** and real funds.

<Demo.Container name="Add Funds" showBadge={false}>
  <SignInWithTempo stepNumber={1} />
  <DepositToTempoWallet stepNumber={2} last />
</Demo.Container>

To add the same button to your app, request [`wallet_deposit`](/docs/guide/getting-funds#open-funding-with-the-accounts-sdk) with the Accounts SDK. The request resolves before card or bank funding completes, so it doesn't mean the account is funded yet.

### With the CLI

After [setting up Tempo Wallet in the CLI](/docs/cli/wallet), run:

```bash
tempo wallet fund
```

### With an agent

Paste this into your agent to set up Tempo Wallet and add funds:

:::code-group
```bash [Claude Code]
claude -p "Read https://tempo.xyz/skills/wallet/SKILL.md and fund my Tempo Wallet"
```
```bash [Amp]
amp --execute "Read https://tempo.xyz/skills/wallet/SKILL.md and fund my Tempo Wallet"
```
```bash [Codex CLI]
codex exec "Read https://tempo.xyz/skills/wallet/SKILL.md and fund my Tempo Wallet"
```
:::

## Add a card or bank onramp to your app

Onramp partners turn a card or bank payment into stablecoins sent to the account's address. [Stripe Crypto Onramp](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#stripe), [MoonPay](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#moonpay), and [Mt Pelerin](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#mt-pelerin) each offer a buy flow you embed in your app or link to. [Bridge](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#bridge) gives customers virtual bank accounts for incoming funds. [Payments & ramps](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps) lists every partner.

## Bring stablecoins from another chain

[Tempo Routes](/docs/routes) moves stablecoins from supported networks to a Tempo account. Make a [transfer](/docs/routes/transfers) your app authorizes, or give customers a [Routes deposit address](/docs/routes/deposits) they fund from an external wallet.

## Confirm the funds arrived

Read an account's stablecoin balances with the Tempo API. Enter the address, choose Moderato (`chainId=42431`) or mainnet (`chainId=4217`), and click **Try**. It needs no wallet connection or signing key.

<OpenApi.Playground operationId="getAddressBalances" query="chainId=42431" anchors={false} />

A balance shows what the account holds, not which payment arrived. Before you credit a specific payment, [verify the incoming transfer](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment): if the account has a [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies), Tempo holds payments it doesn't accept instead of crediting them. To take deposits for many customers into one account, see [Hold customer funds in an omnibus account](/docs/accounts/custodians/omnibus-accounts).

## Get test funds

On Moderato testnet, the [Tempo Faucet](/docs/guide/getting-funds#testnet-funds) sends OUSD and other test stablecoins. On the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet), its [faucet](/docs/accounts/create#fund-an-account-on-devnet) sends test stablecoins, including pathUSD, to any address.
