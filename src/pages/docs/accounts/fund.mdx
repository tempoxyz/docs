## Add money with Tempo Wallet

Select **Connect Tempo Wallet**, then **Add funds**, and choose a funding method. The wallet shows the amount and any fees before you confirm. This demo uses **Tempo Mainnet** and real funds.

<Demo.Container name="Add Funds" showBadge={false}>
  <SignInWithTempo stepNumber={1} />
  <DepositToTempoWallet stepNumber={2} last />
</Demo.Container>

To add the same button to your app, request [`wallet_deposit`](/docs/guide/getting-funds#open-funding-with-the-accounts-sdk) with the Accounts SDK.

## Check the balance

Enter the account's address, choose Moderato (`chainId=42431`) or mainnet (`chainId=4217`), and click **Try**.

<OpenApi.Playground operationId="getAddressBalances" query="chainId=42431" anchors={false} />

Card and bank funding can land after the wallet or onramp says it's done. To credit a specific payment, [verify the incoming transfer](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment) rather than the balance.
