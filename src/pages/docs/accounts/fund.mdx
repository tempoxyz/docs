# Add funds

A Tempo account holds stablecoins at its address. Choose how money reaches that address: from another Tempo account, a card or bank, or another chain.

## Receive from another Tempo account

A sender needs only the account's address. Share it, then [verify the incoming transfer](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment) before you credit the payment.

## Add funds with Tempo Wallet

[Tempo Wallet](/docs/guide/getting-funds#tempo-wallet) has a built-in onramp and funding from other chains. To open it from your app, add a funding button with the Accounts SDK, which requests [`wallet_deposit`](/docs/guide/getting-funds#open-funding-with-the-accounts-sdk) for the connected account.

## Pay by card or bank

Onramp partners turn a card or bank payment into stablecoins sent to the account. [MoonPay](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#moonpay) and [Mt Pelerin](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#mt-pelerin) embed a hosted buy flow, and [Bridge](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#bridge) offers virtual bank accounts for incoming funds; businesses on [Stripe](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#stripe) can receive and hold stablecoins with Stripe Treasury. See [Payments & ramps](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps) for the full list.

## Bring funds from another chain

[Tempo Routes](/docs/routes) moves stablecoins from supported networks to a Tempo account. Make a [transfer](/docs/routes/transfers) your app authorizes, or give customers a [Routes deposit address](/docs/routes/deposits) to fund from an external wallet.

## Accept only what you want

A [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) sets which stablecoins and senders can pay the account. Tempo holds anything else instead of crediting it, so check the recipient before you credit a payment.

## Give each customer a deposit address

If you run one omnibus account for many customers, give each customer a [virtual address](/docs/accounts/virtual-addresses). Every deposit lands in your account, and its transfer events name the customer it belongs to.

## Get test funds

For development, the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet) has a faucet that sends test stablecoins, including pathUSD, to any address. On testnet, use the [Tempo Faucet](/docs/guide/getting-funds#testnet-funds) for OUSD and other test stablecoins.
