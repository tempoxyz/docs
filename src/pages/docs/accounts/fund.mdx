# Add funds

Stablecoins reach a Tempo account at its address, from another Tempo account, a card or bank, or another chain.

Pick the path that matches where the money starts: another Tempo account, Tempo Wallet, a card or bank, or another chain. Whichever path you use, confirm the transfer reached the account before you credit the payment, because a [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) can hold funds instead of crediting them.

## Get test funds

For development, the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet) has a [faucet](/docs/accounts/create#fund-an-account-on-devnet) that sends test stablecoins, including pathUSD, to any address. On Moderato testnet, use the [Tempo Faucet](/docs/guide/getting-funds#testnet-funds) for OUSD and other test stablecoins. The rest of this page covers real funds.

## Receive from another Tempo account

A sender needs only the account's address. Share it, then [verify the incoming transfer](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment) before you credit the payment.

## Add funds with Tempo Wallet

[Tempo Wallet](https://wallet.tempo.xyz) lets the user fund the account through its built-in onramp or from other chains. Select **Connect Tempo Wallet** below, then **Add funds** to open the wallet's funding popup and choose a method. The wallet shows the amount and any fees before you confirm.

This demo uses **Tempo Mainnet** and real funds.

A resolved request doesn't mean the account is funded yet, so [verify the incoming transfer](/docs/guide/payments/accept-a-payment) before you credit the payment.

## Pay by card or bank

Onramp partners turn a card or bank payment into stablecoins sent to the account. [Stripe Crypto Onramp](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#stripe), [MoonPay](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#moonpay) and [Mt Pelerin](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#mt-pelerin) each offer a buy flow you embed in your app or link to, and [Bridge](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps#bridge) offers virtual bank accounts for incoming funds. Businesses on Stripe can also hold stablecoin balances with Stripe Treasury. See [Payments & ramps](/docs/partners/payments-and-ramps) for the full list.

## Bring funds from another chain

[Tempo Routes](/docs/routes) moves stablecoins from supported networks to a Tempo account. Make a [transfer](/docs/routes/transfers) your app authorizes, or give customers a [Routes deposit address](/docs/routes/deposits) to fund from an external wallet.

## Accept only what you want

A [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) sets which stablecoins and senders can pay the account. Tempo holds anything else instead of crediting it, so check the recipient before you credit a payment.

## Give each customer a deposit address

A [virtual address](/docs/accounts/virtual-addresses) lets one omnibus account take deposits for many customers. Give each customer their own virtual address. Every deposit lands in your account, and its transfer events name the deposit address it was sent to, so you credit the customer you gave that address.
