# Custodians

If your funds sit with a custodian, the custodian holds the account key and sends transactions for you.

A custody address on Tempo is a [single-key account](/docs/accounts/how-it-works#address) whose key the custodian controls, behind its own approvals. Plain stablecoin transfers work as on any EVM chain. What else you get depends on whether your custodian sends [Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction), which add a fee token you choose, fee sponsorship, memos, and access keys. Some, such as Fireblocks, do; others support only withdrawals and choose the fee token themselves.

## What your custodian controls

Your custodian holds the key and decides, through its own policies and approvals, which transactions it signs. Custodians such as Fireblocks and Anchorage often hold many clients' funds in one omnibus wallet.

<span id="what-custodians-support-on-tempo" />

## What Tempo adds, by custodian

[Fireblocks](https://developers.fireblocks.com) sends Tempo Transactions with a fee token you choose. Its Tempo transfer API, in beta, also offers parallel and expiring nonces, fee sponsorship from another vault in the same workspace, and memos. A vault can authorize an [access key](/docs/accounts/access-keys) with a contract call to the [Account Keychain](/docs/protocol/transactions/AccountKeychain), and [raw signing](https://developers.fireblocks.com/docs/raw-signing) covers the rest, behind a policy rule that allows it.

Other custodians may support only withdrawals of listed stablecoins, and pick the fee token themselves. Ask yours whether it sends Tempo Transactions or signs raw hashes. [Partners](/docs/partners/wallets#custodial--institutional) lists custodians that support Tempo.

## What we recommend

<span id="choose-the-fee-token-for-custody-addresses" />

### Set a default fee token on each custody address

Set a [default fee token](/docs/accounts/fees/fee-tokens#set-a-default-fee-token) on each custody address, usually the stablecoin it holds, such as OUSD. Tempo has no gas token: every transaction pays its fee in a USD stablecoin. Without a default, a stablecoin transfer pays its fee in the stablecoin it sends, and other calls pay in pathUSD ([fee token precedence](/docs/accounts/fees#fee-token-precedence)).

<span id="attribute-deposits-in-an-omnibus-wallet" />

### Give each client a deposit address

Give each client a [deposit address](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses) that forwards to the omnibus wallet, so each deposit's transfer event names the client it belongs to. For payments out, add a memo with your internal reference.

<span id="control-what-arrives" />

### Accept only the stablecoins and senders you expect

A [receive policy](/docs/accounts/policies/receive-policies) lets a custody address accept only the stablecoins and senders you allow. Tempo holds anything else for recovery instead of crediting it.
