# Signing methods

A signer is a key that a Tempo account lists as able to approve. Each approval is a signature that Tempo checks on chain against the account's signers and threshold. Tempo supports three signing methods, and an account can mix them.

<span id="tempo-account-signer-types" />
<span id="tempo-sign-in-methods" />

## Tempo signing methods

| Method | Where the key lives | What it trusts | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Private keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys) | A CLI, hardware, or browser wallet, or your KMS or HSM | Whoever holds the key, and that key's infrastructure | Treasury approvers, backup and recovery keys, servers, and scripts |
| [Device keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/device-keys) | The device's secure hardware or browser, non-extractable | The customer's device | Everyday payments within limits, with no prompt |
| [Passkeys](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) | The customer's authenticator, synced or on a security key | The customer's device and passkey provider | Customer approvals and signer changes |

None of them depends on an outside service: the key that signs is the key the account lists.

## Combine signing methods

A signer approves at its weight, and a transaction executes once approving weights reach the account's threshold. Mix methods and holders so no single party reaches the threshold alone:

- **Customer accounts:** a passkey at weight 2, a backup key the customer keeps offline at weight 1, and a recovery provider's key at weight 1, at threshold 2. The passkey approves alone, and the two keys together recover it.
- **Treasuries:** private keys in separate wallets or HSMs, such as 2 of 3.
- **Everyday payments:** a device key or a server key as an [access key](/docs/accounts/access-keys), which pays alone within limits and never counts toward the threshold.

See [Signing thresholds](/docs/accounts/security-policies) for recommended setups, and the [guides](/docs/accounts/guides/self-custody) for walkthroughs.

<span id="who-can-sign-in-as-a-customer" />

## Who can sign for a customer

Only the keys an account lists can approve, and only at their weights. Tempo has no admin key or override, so your app, a wallet provider, or a recovery provider can approve only with the signers it holds. Keep any signer you hold below the threshold on its own, and pair it with a passkey the customer holds.

## Signing method limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Signers per account | 1 to 48, each a secp256k1, P-256, or WebAuthn key |
| Signatures per transaction | Up to 8 |
| Passkey prompt | `NotAllowedError` when the customer cancels or the page isn't served over HTTPS |
| Access keys toward the threshold | Never |
