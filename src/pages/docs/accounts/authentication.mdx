# Signing methods

A Tempo account approves with private keys, device keys, or passkeys, and one account can mix all three. Choose by who must hold the key: a passkey keeps the account with the customer, while a server or provider key adds a party who can approve.

<span id="what-a-signer-is" />

## What a signer is

A signer is a key that an account lists as able to approve, at a weight. Each approval is a signature that Tempo checks on chain against the account's signers, and a transaction executes once approving weights reach the account's [threshold](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds). A signing method is the kind of key behind a signer: where it lives and what it trusts.

## Tempo signing methods

| Method | Where the key lives | What it trusts | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Private keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys) | A CLI, hardware, or browser wallet, or your KMS or HSM | Whoever holds the key, and that key's infrastructure | Treasury approvers, backup and recovery keys, servers, and scripts |
| [Device keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/device-keys) | The device's secure hardware or browser, non-extractable | The customer's device | Everyday payments within limits, with no prompt |
| [Passkeys](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) | The customer's authenticator, synced or on a security key | The customer's device and passkey provider | Customer approvals and signer changes |

None of them depends on an outside service: the key that signs is the key the account lists.

## Combine signing methods

Mix methods and holders so no single party reaches the threshold alone:

- **Customer accounts:** a passkey that approves alone, plus a backup key and a recovery provider's key that together recover it.
- **Treasuries:** private keys in separate wallets or HSMs, such as 2 of 3.
- **Everyday payments:** a device key or a server key as an [access key](/docs/accounts/access-keys), which pays alone within limits and never counts toward the threshold.

See [Recommended signer setups](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds#recommended-signer-setups) for weights, and the [guides](/docs/accounts/guides/users) for walkthroughs.

<span id="who-can-sign-in-as-a-customer" />
<span id="who-can-sign-for-a-customer" />

Only the keys an account lists can approve, and only at their weights. Tempo has no admin key or override, so your app, a wallet provider, or a recovery provider can approve only with the signers it holds. Keep any signer you hold below the threshold on its own, and pair it with a passkey the customer holds.

## Signing method limits

Each method's page lists its limits. Limits on signers per account, total weight, and approvals per transaction apply to every method; see [Signing threshold limits](/docs/accounts/policies/signing-thresholds#signing-threshold-limits).

| Method | Main limit |
| --- | --- |
| [Private keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys#private-key-limits) | Anyone with the key approves at its full weight. |
| [Device keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/device-keys#device-key-limits) | A browser key is gone if the customer clears site data. |
| [Passkeys](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys#passkey-limits) | A passkey works only on the domain that created it. |
