# Authentication

Every approval for a Tempo account comes from a key or a login. Choose the method that matches who approves.

<span id="tempo-account-signer-types" />

## Tempo sign-in methods

| Method | Approves with | On a new device | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Private keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/private-keys) | A CLI, hardware, or browser wallet, or a KMS or HSM key | Copy the key, or call its service | Treasury approvers, servers, and scripts |
| [Device keys](/docs/accounts/authentication/device-keys) | A key in the device's secure hardware or browser, with no prompt | A new key per device | Everyday payments within limits |
| [Passkeys](/docs/accounts/authentication/passkeys) | Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, Android, or a security key | Synced passkeys follow the customer | Customer approvals and signer changes |
| [OIDC logins](/docs/accounts/authentication/oidc) | An email or SMS code, a social login, enterprise SSO, or Privy | Sign in anywhere | Apps that already sign customers in, and recovery |

OIDC logins depend on [login services](/docs/accounts/authentication/login-services): an identity provider, a salt service, a prover, and a key publisher. The other methods don't.

## Who can sign in as a customer

The ID token names the customer's device key, and Tempo checks its proof on chain, so the salt service and prover can't sign for a customer. Two parties can sign in as any customer they cover: the issuer, and the owner of the key publisher that lists its keys. For email, SMS, and Privy logins, your auth service is that issuer. Pair each login with a passkey the customer holds, and use logins from different issuers, so no single issuer approves alone. See [Policies](/docs/accounts/security-policies).

## Authentication limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Login approval lifetime | At most 10 minutes after the token is issued; viem defaults to 9 minutes from the start of sign-in. After that, approvals fail with `ZkCredentialExpiredError`. |
| Login approvals per transaction | Up to 2 |
| ID token size | Up to 1,024 bytes |
| Signers per login | One per issuer and OAuth client ID. The same person through two providers is two signers. |
| Dropped provider keys | Valid for one hour after removal, so logins in progress still succeed |
| Passkey prompt | `NotAllowedError` when the customer cancels or the page isn't served over HTTPS |
| Access keys toward the threshold | Never |
