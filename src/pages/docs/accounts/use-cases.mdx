# Use cases

Tempo Accounts give each party its own key, and Tempo enforces each key's limits on every transaction.

A customer's passkey, a provider's recovery key, each approver's signer, and your backend's or an agent's access key can each do only what that key allows. Automated payments and agent wallets run on mainnet and testnet with a single-key account. Consumer wallets with several signers, and treasuries, run only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet).

## Consumer wallets

Neither you nor your wallet provider can move the customer's funds. The customer's passkey approves payments alone. Your wallet provider's key and a recovery provider's key approve only together, such as to replace the passkey when the customer loses their phone, and Tempo rejects any payment the provider's key signs alone. See [Embed wallets with a provider](/docs/accounts/guides/users/embed-wallets), [Add a recovery signer](/docs/accounts/guides/users/add-a-recovery-signer), [Recover a user's account](/docs/accounts/guides/users/recover-an-account), and the other [Users guides](/docs/accounts/guides/users).

## Business accounts and treasuries

No one moves the company's stablecoins alone. Each approver holds their own signer, and any two of three approve each payment. A team pays from its own budget, an access key with a daily limit, without asking the approvers each time, and an approver who leaves is removed without moving the funds. See [Set up a company treasury](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/company-treasury), [Give a team a budget](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/team-budget), and [Offboard an approver](/docs/accounts/guides/teams/offboard-an-approver).

## Automated payments

Your backend pays from a customer's account without prompting them, within limits the customer approved once: funding each card swipe, charging a subscription, sweeping idle balances into Earn, or sending scheduled transfers. The customer approves an access key for your backend once, and Tempo holds it to its spending limit, recipients, and expiry, so a leaked backend key can't drain the account. See [Fund a card program](/docs/accounts/guides/automations/fund-a-card-program), [Sweep idle balances into Earn](/docs/accounts/guides/automations/sweep-into-earn), and [Pay approved recipients from a backend](/docs/accounts/guides/automations/pay-out-from-a-backend).

## Agent wallets

An AI agent spends only within the bounds of its own access key: a budget, an expiry, and scopes that list the calls it may make. Tempo rejects any transaction outside those bounds, whatever the agent decides, and you revoke the key to stop it. See [Give an agent a wallet](/docs/accounts/guides/agents/give-an-agent-a-wallet) and [Let an agent pay for APIs](/docs/accounts/guides/agents/pay-for-apis).
