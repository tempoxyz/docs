# Use cases

Find the setup and guides for what you're building.

Account setups for consumer apps, backends and agents, custodians and treasuries, and wallets differ in who holds which key: a customer's passkey, a provider's key, your backend's access key, or an approver's signer. Each can do only what the account allows, and Tempo enforces those limits on every transaction.

Single-key accounts, access keys, fee sponsorship, virtual addresses, and receive policies work on mainnet and testnet. Until T14, accounts with several signers and a threshold run only on the [Tempo Accounts devnet](/docs/accounts/create#connect-to-the-devnet).

<span id="where-should-funds-live" />

## Customer accounts or a company account

Funds live in each customer's own account, or in a company account where your ledger records what each customer is owed. The same choice applies to every balance your product holds:

| Balance | Customer accounts | Company account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stablecoins** | Each customer's account holds its balance. Read it at that address. | One account holds the total. Your ledger records each customer's credit. |
| **[Earn](/docs/earn) positions** | Each customer's account holds its vault shares. The Earn API reports earnings per address. | The company account holds the shares. Your ledger allocates their earnings to customers. |
| **[Zone](/docs/zones) balances** | Each customer reads only their own private balance through account-scoped RPC. | One Zone account holds the funds. Your ledger is the per-customer record. |
| **Build it** | [Apps](/docs/accounts/apps), or [connect the customer's wallet](/docs/accounts/wallets#connect-a-wallet-to-your-app) | [Hold customer funds in an omnibus account](/docs/accounts/custodians/omnibus-accounts) |

A deposit address for a customer identifies incoming payments; it doesn't hold a separate balance or give the customer signing authority over the company account. You can combine the two: customers can have their own accounts for spending while a company account holds Earn positions and pays out.
