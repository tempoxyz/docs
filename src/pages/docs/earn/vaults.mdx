# Vaults

Choose a vault that accepts the stablecoin your customers hold and supports the access and withdrawal requirements of your product.

An Earn [vault](/docs/earn/how-it-works) is a contract that accepts one stablecoin and issues vault shares, units that represent each account's position. The vault earns from one underlying strategy.

<span id="available-vaults" />

## Find a vault

Use the vault address and network agreed for your integration; [contact Tempo](https://tempo.xyz/contact) to discuss which vaults fit. Evaluate where its returns come from, what customers net, how they exit, and who can deposit. A vault's `verified` flag is `true` when the vault is in Tempo's curated Earn registry. It doesn't describe the strategy's risk or guarantee a return.

<span id="inspect-the-selected-vault" />

### Inspect a vault

The demo starts with the Sentora pathUSD vault on Tempo mainnet. Switch to [Moderato](/docs/quickstart/connection-details), Tempo's testnet, to inspect the pathUSD test vault. Each network lists only its approved demo vault, with live configuration from the [Earn API](/docs/api/earn).

<EarnVaultDemo />

Read your vault's configuration with the Earn API `GET /v1/earn/vaults/{vaultId}` endpoint. Add `include=access,apy,capabilities` to return the access, rate, and operation fields this page uses. Save the vault's `id`, its lowercase contract address, together with its network; the same address on another network is a different contract.

## How vaults generate returns

Each vault earns from one strategy. Strategies fall into three categories by the assets they hold:

<EarnStrategyCards>
  <EarnStrategyCard strategy="treasuries" title="U.S. Treasuries" description="Tokenized U.S. Treasury bonds and sovereign debt products." />
  <EarnStrategyCard strategy="credit" title="Institutional credit" description="Tokenized private credit, such as warehouse lending and CLOs." />
  <EarnStrategyCard strategy="lending" title="Collateralized lending" description="Onchain lending pools. Rates move with borrow demand." />
</EarnStrategyCards>

An engine is the contract that connects an Earn vault to its underlying strategy. The vault configuration reports its `engine` and accepted asset (`assetToken`); available strategies vary by network. Engines connect to their source in one of three ways:

| Source | How it connects to Earn |
| --- | --- |
| **Lending** | An engine deposits into an underlying lending vault. |
| **Managed strategies** | An engine connects to a provider-managed vault that allocates funds across its strategy. |
| **Tokenized assets** | An engine accesses an asset, such as tokenized treasuries or private credit, through a supported liquidity pool. |

## What changes a position's value

A vault share's value moves with its strategy's returns, losses, and fees. A `verified` vault carries these risks too.

| Strategy | What moves value |
| --- | --- |
| **U.S. Treasuries** | Interest rates, and the tokenized product's issuer and redemption terms. |
| **Institutional credit** | Borrower repayment. A default reduces value. |
| **Collateralized lending** | Borrow demand sets the rate. A loan whose collateral falls short can cause a loss. |

## What customers net

The vault's `apy` object reports observed rates, measured over a past window. Its `apy.vault` rate already reflects [Earn fees](/docs/earn/how-it-works#fees-and-revenue-share), the share of returns a vault pays to fee recipients such as your platform, and the underlying venue's fees. Show the remaining costs before the account deposits:

| Cost | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Earn fees** | Already in the share price and `apy.vault`. The vault's `state.feesActive` field reports whether it charges them. |
| **Underlying venue fees** | Already in the engine's reported value. |
| **Provider deposit, management, and withdrawal fees** | Read them from the strategy provider's terms before presenting a rate. |
| **Network fees** | Each deposit and withdrawal transaction. |
| **Moving funds from another network** | Each [Routes](/docs/routes) quote, in and out. Routes moves stablecoins between Tempo and other networks. |
| **Rewards** | Reported separately in the `apy` object, under temporary terms. |

An observed APY describes a measurement window; it is not a promised return.

## How customers exit

Check withdrawal support in the vault's `capabilities` object before the first deposit:

| Path | Vault support | Settlement | When liquidity is short |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Immediate redemption** | `capabilities.redeem` | In the redemption transaction | Fails; it does not enter a queue. |
| **Queued withdrawal** | `capabilities.asyncRedeem` | A request, then a later claim | Waits in the queue. |

The vault's `instantLiquidity` field is the amount, in asset base units, that the strategy would release right now across the whole vault. It isn't a quote for one account, and `null` means the engine can't report a reliable value. [Withdraw](/docs/earn/withdraw) covers the exit flow.

## Who can deposit

A vault accepts one stablecoin, and `access.status` reports whether its share token is `open` or `allowlisted` to approved holders. Before enabling a deposit, check the accepted asset, the access status, `state.depositsPaused`, and the operations listed in `capabilities`. The Earn API only reads data; submit deposits and withdrawals with [Viem's Earn actions](/docs/earn/integrate#deposit-into-a-vault) or a direct contract call.

Continue with [Deposit](/docs/earn/integrate).
