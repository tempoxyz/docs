# Vaults

Choose a vault that accepts the stablecoin your customers hold and supports the access and withdrawal requirements of your product.

<span id="available-vaults" />

## Find a vault

Use the vault address and network provided for your integration. Evaluate where its returns come from, what customers net, how they exit, and who can deposit. A vault's `verified` flag marks a curated listing, not a risk rating or a guaranteed return.

## How vaults generate returns

Each vault earns from one strategy. Strategies fall into three categories:

<EarnStrategyCards>
  <EarnStrategyCard strategy="treasuries" title="U.S. Treasuries" description="Tokenized U.S. Treasury bonds and sovereign debt products." />
  <EarnStrategyCard strategy="credit" title="Institutional credit" description="Tokenized private credit, such as warehouse lending and CLOs." />
  <EarnStrategyCard strategy="lending" title="Collateralized lending" description="Onchain lending pools. Rates move with borrow demand." />
</EarnStrategyCards>

An engine is the contract that connects an Earn vault to its underlying strategy. The vault configuration reports its engine and accepted asset; available strategies vary by network.

| Source | How it connects to Earn |
| --- | --- |
| **Lending** | An engine deposits into an underlying lending vault. |
| **Managed strategies** | An engine connects to a provider-managed vault that allocates funds across its strategy. |
| **Tokenized assets** | An engine accesses an asset, such as tokenized treasuries or private credit, through a supported liquidity pool. |

## What changes a position's value

A share's value moves with its strategy's returns, losses, and fees, including for verified vaults.

| Strategy | What moves value |
| --- | --- |
| **U.S. Treasuries** | Interest rates, and the tokenized product's issuer and redemption terms. |
| **Institutional credit** | Borrower repayment. A default reduces value. |
| **Collateralized lending** | Borrow demand sets the rate. A loan whose collateral falls short can cause a loss. |

## What customers net

The observed `apy.vault` already reflects [Earn fees](/docs/earn/how-it-works#fees-and-revenue-share) and the underlying venue's fees. Show the remaining costs before the account deposits:

| Cost | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Earn fees** | Already in the share price and `apy.vault`. `state.feesActive` reports whether the vault charges them. |
| **Underlying venue fees** | Already in the engine's reported value. |
| **Provider deposit, management, and withdrawal fees** | Read them from the provider before presenting a rate. |
| **Network fees** | Each deposit and withdrawal transaction. |
| **Moving funds from another network** | Each [Routes quote](/docs/routes/quotes), in and out. |
| **Rewards** | Reported separately in the `apy` object, under temporary terms. |

An observed APY describes a measurement window; it is not a promised return.

## How customers exit

Check withdrawal support before the first deposit:

| Path | Vault support | Settlement | When liquidity is short |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Immediate redemption** | `capabilities.redeem` | In the redemption transaction | Fails; it does not enter a queue. |
| **Queued withdrawal** | `capabilities.asyncRedeem` | A request, then a later claim | Waits in the queue. |

`instantLiquidity` describes vault-wide liquidity, not a user-specific quote; `null` means a reliable value is unavailable. [Withdraw](/docs/earn/withdraw) covers the exit flow.

## Who can deposit

A vault accepts one stablecoin, and its share token is open or allowlisted to approved holders. Before enabling a deposit, check the accepted asset, the account's eligibility, `state.depositsPaused`, and the operations listed in `capabilities`. Submit deposits and withdrawals with [Viem's Earn actions](/docs/earn/integrate); the Earn API only reads.

Continue with [Deposit](/docs/earn/integrate).
