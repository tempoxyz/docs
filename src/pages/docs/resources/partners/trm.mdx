# TRM on Tempo

Use TRM Wallet Screening to assess an address before authorizing a Tempo payment. TRM also documents Tempo coverage for transaction monitoring, fund tracing, and entity attribution. Choose the products that match your payment review and investigation workflow, then confirm access for your account.

## TRM capabilities for Tempo payments

[TRM's Tempo coverage page](https://www.trmlabs.com/blockchain-coverage/tempo) identifies these workflows:

| Workflow | What TRM provides | Where it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet screening | Address risk scores and counterparty exposure. | Onboarding and payment authorization. |
| Transaction monitoring | Alerts configured around your risk policy. | Reviewing payment activity over time. |
| Fund tracing | Relationships between wallets and flows across supported chains. | Investigating a payment or incident. |
| Entity attribution | Information linking addresses to identified entities. | Understanding a counterparty and reviewing findings. |

[Wallet Screening](https://www.trmlabs.com/crypto-compliance/wallet-screening) supports configurable ownership, counterparty, and indirect exposure checks. It can be used alongside Transaction Monitoring for checks before and after a payment. Select the relevant risk categories and thresholds for your process rather than treating all findings as the same decision.

## Prepare access and a screening policy

Confirm that your TRM account includes the required product and Tempo coverage. The [API documentation portal](https://docs.trmlabs.com/api-reference) exposes documentation for products linked to your account. Public Sanctions and Chainabuse documentation does not establish access to Wallet Screening or its full risk information.

Before integrating, define the address to screen, when a result becomes too old to use, which findings require review, and who can approve an exception. Use the documentation available to your account for authentication, network identifiers, request fields, and error handling.

## Connect TRM results to a payment decision

:::::steps

### Screen before authorizing the payment

Request the address assessment at the decision point in your application. Keep the result, check time, and internal payment reference together.

### Apply your review and escalation rules

Map findings to a defined outcome, such as proceeding, waiting for review, or declining under your policy. Handle service errors and unavailable results explicitly; neither is a clean screen.

### Monitor and investigate the activity you require

If your workflow needs ongoing monitoring or tracing, confirm those product entitlements separately and connect their alerts or cases to your operational records.

:::::

## Keep screening separate from onchain controls

TRM supplies risk information. Your application or operations team decides how that information affects a payment. Screening does not perform every customer check or guarantee that a payment meets applicable obligations.

Tempo's [token and receive controls](/docs/resources/guides/compliance-controls) serve a different purpose: they enforce configured permissions. A TRM result does not update a TIP-403 policy or account receive policy by itself. Implement and authorize any policy changes through your own workflow.

## TRM integration questions

### Does TRM support Tempo?

Yes. TRM documents Tempo support for wallet screening, transaction monitoring, tracing, and entity attribution. Confirm the required product and API access for your account.

### Can I combine wallet screening and transaction monitoring?

Yes. TRM describes using them together for checks before and after transactions. Confirm how your selected products refresh results and produce alerts before relying on continuous coverage.

### Is the public Sanctions API the same as Wallet Screening?

No. TRM lists public Sanctions documentation separately from account-linked API products. Use the documentation and entitlement for the screening product your workflow needs.
