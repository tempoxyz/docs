# Move funds off Tempo

Send stablecoins from Tempo to an exchange, another chain, or another wallet.

## Overview

Tempo Wallet doesn't withdraw to a bank account. To cash out, send stablecoins to an exchange or ramp that supports Tempo, then withdraw to your bank there. To use them on another chain, bridge them.

## Cash out through an exchange

:::::steps

### Get a Tempo deposit address

In Coinbase, receive a supported stablecoin and choose Tempo as the network. Kraken accepts USDT0 deposits on Tempo.

### Send from Tempo Wallet

In Tempo Wallet, select **Send**. Paste the exchange's address as the **Recipient**, choose the stablecoin, and enter the amount. Add a **Note** if the exchange asks for a memo. Select **Send**.

### Withdraw to your bank

When the deposit arrives, sell or withdraw it to your bank on the exchange.

:::::

Send only stablecoins the exchange supports on Tempo. Stablecoins sent on the wrong network or in an unsupported token can be lost.

## Bridge to another chain

A bridge moves stablecoins from Tempo to chains such as Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Solana. To use [Relay](https://relay.link/bridge), connect a wallet that holds your Tempo address, such as MetaMask or Rabby. Choose **Tempo** as the source chain and pick the destination. Other bridges are in the [bridge directory](/docs/partners/bridges).

## Use a wallet with a built-in off-ramp

Some wallets that support Tempo sell stablecoins for local currency. [Bridge Wallet by Mt Pelerin](https://www.mtpelerin.com/bridge-wallet) supports cashing out supported assets. Send your stablecoins to its Tempo address first.
