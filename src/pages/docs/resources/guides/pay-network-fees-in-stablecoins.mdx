# Pay fees in stablecoins

Tempo has no gas token. Every network fee is paid in a USD stablecoin.

## Overview

A network fee is what a transaction pays to be included in a block. On Tempo, it's priced in USD and paid in a USD stablecoin you already hold, such as OUSD, USDC.e, or USDT0. You never need to buy ETH or another token to pay it.

## Which stablecoin pays

Tempo picks the fee token in this order:

1. **Your choice for the transaction.** Some apps let you pick it.
2. **Your account's default.** You set it once.
3. **The stablecoin you send.** A transfer of USDC.e pays its fee in USDC.e.
4. **The stablecoin you swap from.** This applies to a single swap.
5. **pathUSD.** The fallback for every other transaction.

When an app or fee payer covers the fee, its own default applies instead of yours. The fee token must be a USD stablecoin. Tempo rejects a transaction when your account can't cover the maximum fee in that token. It doesn't try another token.

## Fees in Tempo Wallet

Tempo Wallet picks the fee token for you. Each review screen shows a **Fee** row in USD. When an app or Tempo covers the fee, the row says **Sponsored**, and you pay nothing.

## Fees in MetaMask and other wallets

Standard wallets follow the same order. Keep a small balance of the stablecoin you send. Wallets show a large placeholder in place of a native balance. It isn't money you hold.

## When a transaction fails for fees

Check that you hold enough of the fee token. If the fee falls back to pathUSD and you hold none, swap a little into pathUSD. In Tempo Wallet, select **Swap**. Or [set a default fee token](/docs/accounts/fees/fee-tokens#set-a-default-fee-token) you hold.
