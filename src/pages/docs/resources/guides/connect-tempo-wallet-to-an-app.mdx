# Connect Tempo Wallet to an app

Sign in to apps and agents with your Tempo Wallet, then disconnect them when you're done.

## Overview

[Tempo Wallet](https://wallet.tempo.xyz) is a web wallet you open with your email or a passkey. An app connects to it through a **Sign in with Tempo** window. If the app wants to pay from your account, you approve a spending limit first.

## Connect to an app

:::::steps

### Select Sign in with Tempo

In the app, select **Sign in with Tempo**. A Tempo Wallet window opens.

### Sign in

Enter your email and the 6-digit code it receives, or select **Continue with passkey**. A new email creates a wallet.

### Review the request

If the app asks to spend, the window says it will be authorized to spend from your account. It lists the spending limit, network fees, and expiry. Select **Approve** to connect, or **Reject**.

:::::

[Set a spending limit](/docs/resources/guides/set-an-agent-spending-limit) explains each line on the approval screen.

## Connect an AI agent

:::::steps

### Open Agent payments

In Tempo Wallet, open the account menu and select **Agent payments**.

### Copy the command

Choose your agent: Claude, Codex, Amp, OpenCode, or Cursor. Copy the command and run it in the agent. For Claude Code:

```bash
claude "Read https://tempo.xyz/SKILL.md and connect to my Tempo wallet"
```

### Approve in the browser

The agent opens Tempo Wallet. Check that the device code matches your terminal, then approve the spending limit.

:::::

From a terminal, `tempo wallet login` starts the same flow. The [`tempo wallet` reference](/docs/cli/wallet) lists its other commands.

## Disconnect an app

Sign in to Tempo Wallet with your passkey, then open [wallet.tempo.xyz/apps](https://wallet.tempo.xyz/apps). It lists connected apps and CLI keys with their spending limits. Select **Disconnect** on an app to revoke its keys. Revoked keys can't spend.
