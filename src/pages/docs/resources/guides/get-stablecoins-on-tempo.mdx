# Get stablecoins on Tempo

Move stablecoins onto Tempo from another chain, an exchange, or another Tempo account.

## Overview

Tempo has no native token. Your balance is held in stablecoins, such as OUSD, USDC.e, and USDT0. Anyone can send stablecoins to a Tempo address, with nothing to set up first.

Pick a path by where your money is now:

- **On another chain.** Deposit it into [Tempo Wallet](https://wallet.tempo.xyz), or use a bridge.
- **On an exchange.** Withdraw it and choose Tempo as the network.
- **In another Tempo account.** Share your address and receive it.

## Deposit from another chain

Tempo Wallet gives you a deposit address on the chain your funds are on. It converts what you send and adds OUSD to your Tempo balance.

:::::steps

### Open the deposit screen

Sign in at [wallet.tempo.xyz](https://wallet.tempo.xyz) with your email or a passkey. A new email creates a wallet. Select **Deposit**, then **Deposit crypto**.

### Choose the network and token

Pick the **Network** and **Token** you're sending from.

| Network | Tokens |
| --- | --- |
| Base | USDC, OUSD, ETH, WETH, cbBTC, SOL, WBTC |
| Ethereum | USDC, USDT, OUSD, ETH, WETH, WBTC |
| Arbitrum | USDC, USDT, ETH |
| Optimism | USDC, USDT, ETH, WETH |
| Solana | USDC, SOL |
| Unichain | USDC, ETH |
| Abstract | USDC, ETH |

### Send to the deposit address

Copy the **Deposit address** or scan its QR code. Send only the token and network you selected. Select **Done**. The wallet shows **Deposit complete** when the OUSD arrives.

:::::

Deposits from other chains go to Tempo mainnet only. [Relay](/docs/guide/bridge-relay) routes them.

<span id="withdraw-from-an-exchange" />

## Withdraw from an exchange

Some exchanges send stablecoins on Tempo directly:

- **Coinbase.** Select Tempo as the network when you send a supported stablecoin.
- **Kraken.** Withdraw USDT0 on Tempo.

Paste your Tempo address as the destination. Check that the exchange shows Tempo as the network before you confirm.

## Bridge from another chain

A bridge moves stablecoins from another chain to any Tempo address, including one in MetaMask or Rabby. To use [Relay](https://relay.link/bridge), choose your source chain and token, then set **Tempo** as the destination.

Other bridges, such as LayerZero, Bungee, and Across, are in the [bridge directory](/docs/partners/bridges).

## Receive from another Tempo account

In Tempo Wallet, select **Receive**, then copy your **Wallet address** or show its QR code. Only send supported stablecoins on Tempo to it.

## Get test stablecoins

The [Moderato testnet faucet](/docs/quickstart/faucet) gives you free test stablecoins. They have no value and work only on testnet.
