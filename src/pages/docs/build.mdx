## Add payment controls

- [Attach transfer memos](/docs/guide/payments/transfer-memos) to associate payments with invoices or other records.
- [Use virtual addresses](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses) to assign a deposit address to each customer.
- [Sponsor transaction fees](/docs/guide/payments/sponsor-user-fees) on behalf of your users.
- [Choose a fee token](/docs/guide/payments/pay-fees-in-any-stablecoin) to pay transaction fees with a supported stablecoin.
- [Configure receive policies](/docs/guide/payments/configure-receive-policies) to control accepted tokens and senders, and handle blocked transfers.

## Connect and fund your application

Start with [Accounts](/docs/accounts) to connect a wallet and add payment controls. Use [Routes](/docs/routes) to fund accounts across networks, or the separate [testnet faucet](/docs/quickstart/faucet) for development. For chain configuration and EVM behavior, follow the [network integration guide](/docs/quickstart/integrate-tempo).

For backend payment activity and event notifications, start with the [Tempo API](/docs/api).
