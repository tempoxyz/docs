# Tempo EVM

Tempo EVM is Tempo’s Ethereum-compatible execution environment, built for payments. Use Solidity and familiar Ethereum tools, with account, token, and payment features built into the chain.

## Core ideas

| Idea | What it lets you build |
| --- | --- |
| **Stablecoin fees** | Pay transaction fees in supported USD-denominated TIP-20 tokens, without managing a separate gas token. Your app can [sponsor fees](/docs/guide/payments/sponsor-user-fees) for customers. |
| **Payment capacity** | [Payment lanes](/docs/protocol/blockspace/payment-lane-specification) reserve capacity for eligible payments alongside general-purpose applications. |
| **Flexible transactions** | [Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction) support atomic batches, fee sponsorship, and concurrent payments. [Access keys](/docs/accounts/access-keys) give apps limited, revocable signing permissions. |
| **Native tokens and exchange** | [TIP-20 Tokens](/docs/network/tokens) provide issuance and transfer controls. The [stablecoin exchange](/docs/guide/stablecoin-dex) supplies shared onchain liquidity for supported pairs. |
| **Deposits and reconciliation** | [Customer deposit addresses](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses) credit one registered account while preserving customer attribution. [Transfer memos](/docs/guide/payments/transfer-memos) carry payment references. |

Your application manages customer records, transaction submission, accounting, and screening decisions. Tempo enforces the configured transaction, permission, and token rules. [Zones](/docs/zones) add private balances through a separate integration.

## Use your existing tools

Standard EVM transactions work for basic transfers and contract calls. Use Tempo Transactions when you need batching, fee sponsorship, access keys, or concurrent payments. Review [EVM differences](/docs/quickstart/evm-compatibility) before porting an application, especially native-balance checks and fee-token selection.

Start with [RPC endpoints and chain IDs](/docs/quickstart/connection-details), choose an [API or SDK](/docs/tools), or find a [wallet or infrastructure provider](/docs/ecosystem). Provider support varies by feature.

## Accounts and payments

[Accounts](/docs/accounts) covers creating accounts, connecting wallets, sending payments, and confirming delivery. These walkthroughs remain in the Accounts tab:

- [Customer deposit addresses](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses) — try address derivation and a live test deposit into a shared account.
- [Sponsor fees](/docs/guide/payments/sponsor-user-fees) — send a payment with your application paying the fee.
- [Manage admin keys](/docs/accounts/admin-keys) — authorize and revoke a test account's delegated administrator.

Use the [integration walkthrough](/docs/accounts/integration) to connect signing and payment confirmation to your application records, or start from the [complete payment examples](/docs/guide/payments/send-a-payment/examples).

## Tokens and exchange

Use [TIP-20 Tokens](/docs/network/tokens) for token behavior and [Exchange](/docs/guide/stablecoin-dex) for swaps and the orderbook.

- [Mint stablecoins](/docs/guide/issuance/mint-stablecoins) — create or load a token, grant minting authority, and mint test funds.
- [Manage your stablecoin](/docs/guide/issuance/manage-stablecoin) — try supply caps, roles, policies, and transfer pauses.
- [Provide liquidity](/docs/guide/stablecoin-dex/providing-liquidity) — approve funds, place an order, inspect it, and cancel it.
