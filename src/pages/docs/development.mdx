# Tempo EVM

Tempo is a blockchain for payments. Tempo EVM is its Ethereum-compatible execution environment: use Solidity contracts, Ethereum JSON-RPC, and familiar tools like Foundry, Viem, and Wagmi to build with stablecoins.

[Send your first test payment →](/get-started/quickstart) · [RPC endpoints and chain IDs →](/docs/quickstart/connection-details)

</DocsProductOverview>

The chain handles more of the work a payment application needs. Pay transaction fees in supported stablecoins, sponsor fees for customers, attach references to transfers, and give an application limited signing permissions. Native token contracts provide issuance and transfer controls, while payment lanes reserve capacity for eligible payments.

For example, a payout service can send payments concurrently, cover transaction fees, and include an invoice reference with each transfer. Your application still decides who to pay and keeps its own records; Tempo executes the transfers and enforces the permissions you configure.

## Payment features

Start with a transfer or contract call, then add the capabilities your application needs. Standard EVM transactions work for basic transfers and calls; use [Tempo Transactions](/docs/guide/tempo-transaction) for features such as batching, fee sponsorship, and access keys.
