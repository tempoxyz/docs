# T12 Network Upgrade

T12 makes repeated payments easier for wallets and bridges, expands payment-channel support, and improves compatibility and swap quotes.

## Timeline

| Network | Planned activation |
|---------|--------------------|
| Testnet | October 1, 2026 — time TBD |
| Mainnet | October 6, 2026 — time TBD |

<span id="t12-upgrade-overview" />

## Overview

- **Easier repeated payments:** lets wallets and bridges give separate identifiers to otherwise identical transactions.
- **Payment channels for restricted tokens:** checks whether the payer and recipient are allowed to send and receive the token.
- **Relaxed ABI validation:** accepts correctly formatted calls to Tempo's built-in contracts with extra data at the end.
- **More reliable swap quotes:** fixes rounding differences between the amount quoted and the amount calculated during a swap.

## Features

### Easier repeated payments

Wallets and bridges can use a transaction's `nonce` field to give otherwise identical expiring payments different identifiers. This replaces the practice of adding extra access-list entries just to make each transaction unique.

Removing those entries removes one barrier to the [payment lane](/docs/protocol/blockspace/payment-lane-specification); other lane rules still apply.

Read the [specification](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/blob/main/tips/tip-1106.md).

### Payment channels for restricted tokens

Some tokens restrict who can send or receive them. T12 checks permissions for the payer and recipient when adding funds to a payment channel or paying out. The account that temporarily holds the funds no longer needs separate permission from the token issuer.

Read the [specification](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/blob/main/tips/tip-1095.md).

### Relaxed ABI validation

T12 relaxes validation during ABI decoding, the step that reads contract-call data. Tempo's built-in contracts accept extra data at the end of otherwise correctly formatted calls again, restoring compatibility with calls that T11 rejected for this reason.

Read the [specification](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/blob/main/tips/tip-1116.md).

### More reliable swap quotes

T12 makes swap quotes and swaps use the same calculation. This fixes rounding differences that could cause a swap to fail even when the available orders hadn't changed.

Read the [specification](https://github.com/tempoxyz/tempo/blob/main/tips/tip-1088.md).

## Integration impact

- **Wallets and bridges:** to send otherwise identical expiring payments, set different `nonce` values after activation. Replace access-list entries used only for uniqueness, and ensure builders and signing tools keep the nonce you set.
- **Apps using payment channels:** for channels opened at or after T12, configure the recipient's receive policy to accept the token from the payer. For refunds, the payer's policy must accept the token from the reserve, the account holding channel funds. Use channel events alongside token transfers to identify who paid.
- **DEX integrations reading contract storage:** if you read tick liquidity totals directly from storage, switch to `getTickLevel()` or sum the remaining amounts of active orders. Stored tick totals stop updating at T12.
