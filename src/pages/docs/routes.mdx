A route connects a source network and token to a destination network and token. Start with [supported networks and assets](/docs/routes/networks), then quote the exact payment. Routes prepares transfers; your source wallet authorizes and submits them.

## Accept deposits from external wallets

[Accept deposits](/docs/routes/deposits) covers reusable addresses for a specific source network and token. Each incoming deposit has its own delivery status. These differ from [virtual addresses](/docs/guide/payments/virtual-addresses), which attribute payments on Tempo to a shared balance.

## Track the payment through delivery

Read [quotes and fees](/docs/routes/quotes) before requesting authorization. Follow [Track delivery](/docs/routes/delivery) to distinguish source submission, destination receipt, and refunds. Keep pending payments pending until delivery is confirmed.

## Use routed funds across Tempo

After funds arrive, the recipient can [make payments](/docs/payments), [deposit into Earn](/docs/earn/integrate), or [fund a Zone](/docs/zones/deposit). Each product has its own authorization and deposit flow. Direct Routes transfers into Earn vaults or Zones are not implemented.

For exchanges that stay on Tempo, use [Swap stablecoins](/docs/guide/stablecoin-dex/executing-swaps).
