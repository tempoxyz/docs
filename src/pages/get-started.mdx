## Start with an account and a stablecoin

An account is an address with keys that authorize its transactions. It can hold balances in multiple stablecoins. Each stablecoin has its own token contract, and [TIP-20 Tokens](/get-started/stablecoins) share the same payment interface.

Use [Accounts](/docs/accounts) to choose how to create an account and manage its keys. Use [Payments](/docs/payments) to send funds, receive payments, and match transfers to your records.

Your application can sign transactions from a backend, an embedded customer wallet, or a connected external wallet. The example below uses Tempo Wallet to try the payment flow.

## Send your first test payment
