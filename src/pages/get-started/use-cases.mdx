# Use cases

Help customers hold funds, move money, and pay for services with stablecoins. Explore Tempo's core use cases below, then follow a guide to build the experience into your product.

## Embedded finance

Give customers a financial account inside your app, where they can receive payments, keep a stablecoin balance, and send money. Connect the account to the currencies and payment methods your customers already use through Tempo's partner ecosystem.

## Cross-border payments

Pay employees, contractors, suppliers, and customers across countries using stablecoins. A contractor can receive a dollar stablecoin payout and use an offramp partner to withdraw to a bank account in a supported local currency.

## Institutional settlement

Move funds between financial institutions for treasury operations, counterparty payments, and card settlement. Use transfer policies to control who can transact and memos to match payments to your records. Tempo Zones keep balances and account activity inside a Zone private from the public.

  <Card
    title="Treasury and counterparty payments"
